## Idea

One-Time Permission Phone Agent
For private phone tasks, grant a local agent limited data access and permissions, preview its actions, then approve a single execution.

## Product concept

For a single task, users choose which photos, files, or contacts the local agent may access. The agent operates only within that permission scope. Before sending, deleting, or making a payment, it lists the items it will act on and proceeds only after the user confirms.
