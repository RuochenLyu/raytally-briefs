## Idea

Screenshot-to-Reproduction Steps
Capture key screen regions while reproducing a software issue, and get a redacted Markdown ticket with ordered steps and supporting local evidence.

## Product concept

Each time a support agent or tester captures a key screen region, the product recognizes buttons, fields, and error text, then writes them into ordered Markdown reproduction steps. Account details and tokens are redacted on-device, while the ticket retains only the local evidence attached to each step.
