## Idea

Ride Relay Canvas
Cycling groups divide a map design into claimable segments and use their real ride routes to create a shared piece of city art.

## Product concept

Cycling groups draw a design on a map, then divide the route into segments of varying difficulty that members can claim. As members complete their rides, their real routes light up the canvas piece by piece. Any gaps return to the unclaimed pool.
