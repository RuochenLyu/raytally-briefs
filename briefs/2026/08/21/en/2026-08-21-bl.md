## Idea

BL Cross-Site Reading Shelf
A cross-site reading shelf for BL readers that merges alternate listings, tracks progress across apps, and opens new chapters at the first unread page.

## Product concept

The extension records the title and last page whenever a reader opens a chapter, then uses title aliases and cover art to merge versions across sites. When a new chapter is released, its link opens directly to the next unread chapter and prioritizes legitimate access points.
