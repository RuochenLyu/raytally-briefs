Same-Age Blind Reveal Videos
Family members privately submit material from the same age, then receive a reveal video with dual timelines and their genuine reactions.

Family members each submit photos, short videos, and a one-line life update from when they were the same age, without seeing one another’s material beforehand. At the reveal, the app automatically creates a dual timeline and captures each person’s first reaction to the contrast.
