## Idea

Voice Agent Call Replay Console
A self-hosted replay console for failed voice-agent calls, where developers can replay each turn and replace individual components to hear which layer caused the problem.

## Product concept

After a voice-agent call fails, developers can inspect the audio, transcript, prompts, tool calls, and latency on a single timeline. They can select the faulty turn, swap out one component, replay it, and compare the two recordings directly.
