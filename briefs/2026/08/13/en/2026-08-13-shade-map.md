## Idea

Shaded Meeting Point
Before a group heads to a park or outdoor event, they can agree on a meeting spot that will stay shaded throughout their different arrival times.

## Product concept

Once the organizer enters everyone’s arrival times, the product selects a meeting area in the park that will remain shaded by trees for the next hour. As sunlight shifts or someone arrives late, it nudges the meeting point and sends updated walking directions.
