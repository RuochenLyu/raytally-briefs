## Idea

Independent Preschool Visit Notes
After a preschool visit, two parents independently log what they observed, then review where they agree, differ, and what to ask next.

## Product concept

After visiting a preschool, each parent separately records voice notes on teacher-child interactions, safety details, and their child’s reactions. Neither can see the other’s assessment before submitting; afterward, the app brings together agreements, differences, and questions to raise with the school on the next visit.
