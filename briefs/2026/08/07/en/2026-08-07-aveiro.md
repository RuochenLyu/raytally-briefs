## Idea

One Poster, Every Channel
Small venues can turn a single event poster into consistent web pages, emails, and social posts, while managing last-minute changes in one place.

## Product concept

After a small venue uploads an event poster, the product creates a single source-of-truth page for the date, location, ticket price, and registration details. When the organizer changes any field, unpublished content updates automatically, while already published channels receive the appropriate correction task.
