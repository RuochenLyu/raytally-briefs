## Idea

Ten-Minute Earthquake Check for Renters
A quick room-by-room video gives renters earthquake fixes they can handle tonight, prioritized by their impact on escape routes.

## Product concept

Renters film a quick walk-through of their home, and the product flags items that could tip over, fall, or block an exit along escape routes. It sorts fixes into three groups: steps they can take tonight, issues to raise with the landlord, and items that require purchased anchors; after filming again, they can check whether the risks are gone.
