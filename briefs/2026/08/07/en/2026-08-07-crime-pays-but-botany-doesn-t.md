## Idea

Roadside Plant Fieldcraft
Photograph unfamiliar plants on a walk, follow safe observation prompts to identify them, and learn why they grow where they do.

## Product concept

Photograph an unfamiliar plant on a walk, and the app first gives you a safe observation to make in the field, such as checking the leaf margins or looking for an inflorescence. Only after you complete it does it reveal likely species and ecological relationships; for poisonous or protected plants, it offers viewing-from-a-distance guidance only.
