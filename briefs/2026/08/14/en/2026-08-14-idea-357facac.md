## Idea

Send Me My Next Book
When you want to support someone with a book, send one directly from their public shelf with a note on why you recommend it—no address required.

## Product concept

Readers add books they want to a public shelf. Gift-givers simply choose a title and write a brief note explaining the recommendation. The platform reserves the book, while bookstores fulfill the order anonymously.
