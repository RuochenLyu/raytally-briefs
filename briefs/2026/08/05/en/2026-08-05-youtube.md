## Idea

YouTube Original Audio Lock
When a YouTube video opens, this extension automatically restores the original audio track so viewers who do not want automatic dubbing do not have to disable it video by video.

## Product concept

Before a video starts playing, the extension detects automatic dubbing and switches back to the original audio track according to the user’s rules. Subtitles remain available, and exceptions can be set for specific channels. If no original audio is available, it makes that clear.
