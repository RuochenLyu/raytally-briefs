## Idea

Intent Mode for Short Videos
ScrollToll asks users to set an intention before opening short-video apps, counts only mindless recommendation-feed scrolling, and returns them to their original task when they hit the limit.

## Product concept

Before opening a short-video app, users choose whether they are searching, replying to messages, or just browsing. Only consecutive swipes through recommended videos count against the limit. When the limit is reached, the page shows the original purpose and takes the user back to search or messages.
