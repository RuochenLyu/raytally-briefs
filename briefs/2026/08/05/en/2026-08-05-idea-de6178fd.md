## Idea

Role-Specific Casting Alerts
Track a role page after submitting a voice-over audition and receive a clear alert if its deadline moves up, casting is announced, or the project is canceled.

## Product concept

After submitting an audition, voice actors save the role page and the product periodically compares page snapshots. If a deadline moves up, casting is completed, or the project is canceled, the alert identifies the specific role. Closed auditions are archived automatically.
