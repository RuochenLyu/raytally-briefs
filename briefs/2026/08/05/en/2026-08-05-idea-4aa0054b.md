## Idea

One-Time Job Application Profile
Upload a résumé once, then have repeated job-application fields filled in automatically while reviewing only new or conflicting questions.

## Product concept

After saving a structured work history in the browser, repeated fields are filled in automatically across job application forms. The user is asked to confirm only new questions, conflicting answers, or unusual formats. Before submission, the tool generates a one-page review summary.
