## Idea

An On-the-Ground Itinerary for Visiting Friends
Before a friend visits, arrange your saved places into a walkable itinerary that adapts on the day to the weather, their energy, and the time left.

## Product concept

City locals pin their favorite restaurants and attractions, then add their visiting friend’s accommodation, available time, and food preferences. The itinerary replans when it rains, they get tired, or their schedule shrinks, while stops the host marks as must-sees are prioritized.
