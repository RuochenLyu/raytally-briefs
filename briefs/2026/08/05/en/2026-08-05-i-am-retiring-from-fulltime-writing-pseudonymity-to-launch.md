## Idea

Pen Name Succession Trust
Lets pseudonymous creators set conditional handoffs of their accounts, work, and identity credentials when they retire or die.

## Product concept

Pseudonymous authors encrypt and store identity credentials, a catalog of their work, and recovery keys, then define conditions that trustees must jointly trigger. A successor can prove that a statement comes from the same pen name without revealing the person’s real identity. Different materials can be designated for public release, platform verification only, or permanent sealing.
