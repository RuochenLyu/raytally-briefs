## Idea

Illustration Publishing Ledger
Before posting an illustration, drag it in to see whether it has been shared before, when it was posted, and the original post.

## Product concept

Illustrators drag in an image they are about to post, and the product uses a visual fingerprint to search for earlier posts. It returns the original post’s date and link even for cropped, compressed, or watermarked versions. Artists can add character, series, and commission tags to each piece.
