## Idea

Offline Travel Translation Guide
When there is no connection while traveling, use the camera or voice to describe the problem in front of you and get practical local-language guidance right away.

## Product concept

Travelers download language packs before their trip, then photograph menus and machine interfaces when they reach an offline counter, subway station, or remote area. Combining what the camera sees with spoken questions, the app tells them where to point, which button to press, or what to say. Recognition and translation run entirely on the phone.
