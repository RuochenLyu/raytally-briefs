## Idea

One-Minute Human Takeover
When a voice agent gets stuck, it routes the live issue to the right human for resolution, then resumes the call with the answer.

## Product concept

When a voice agent fails to understand the caller for two consecutive turns, or the caller explicitly asks for a person, it passes the current summary to the right human. Once the human resolves the issue, the agent resumes the call with the outcome.
