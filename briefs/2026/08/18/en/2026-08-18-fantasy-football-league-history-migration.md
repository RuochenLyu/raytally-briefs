## Idea

Fantasy League Archive Migration
When moving a league to a new platform, commissioners connect their old and new accounts, review a complete historical copy, then transfer years of results and draft records in one move.

## Product concept

After connecting accounts on the old and new platforms, a fantasy football commissioner receives a browsable shadow version of the league to review. Once everything checks out, they can migrate seasons, drafts, rosters, and honors records.
