## Idea

Block Reason Notes
When you block an account for phishing or harassment, it automatically keeps a private record of why, so you can immediately remember the reason if you encounter it again.

## Product concept

When you block an account on X, the extension saves the triggering post along with a private one-line note. If that account appears again later, the original reason surfaces in place on the page.
