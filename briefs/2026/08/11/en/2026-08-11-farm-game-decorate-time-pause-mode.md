## Idea

Farm Game Decorating Pause
A farm-game mod that pauses the schedule while players decorate rooms or farms, so designing does not cost them an in-game day.

## Product concept

When players pick up furniture, move buildings, or lay flooring, the mod freezes the day-night cycle and quest timers. Time resumes when they leave decorating mode, and materials are not consumed until draft placements are confirmed.
