## Idea

Task Breakpoint Capsule
When work is interrupted, it saves the task context automatically so users can restore it in one click and see what changed while they were away.

## Product concept

When a meeting or urgent message interrupts work, the desktop automatically saves open windows, terminal sessions, and contextual clues. On return, the user opens a breakpoint card to restore the workspace and see changes made to shared documents while they were away.
