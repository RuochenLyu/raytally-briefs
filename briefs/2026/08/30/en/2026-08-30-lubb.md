## Idea

Sleep in Sync
As long-distance partners get ready for bed, they can briefly share softened live heartbeats through a bedside touch pad, creating the sensation that the other person is beside them.

## Product concept

Once both long-distance partners confirm they are ready for sleep, bedside touch pads briefly transmit softened versions of each other’s live heartbeat. Either person can stop at any time, and signals are not saved by default.
