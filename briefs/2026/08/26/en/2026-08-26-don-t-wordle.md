## Idea

Forbidden-Word Voice Game
In a group voice chat, players avoid their own forbidden words while trying to bait everyone else into saying theirs, with automatic calls and instant replays.

## Product concept

Friends join a voice room and each receive a secret forbidden word. When someone says theirs, the system clips the surrounding audio for playback and adjudication.
