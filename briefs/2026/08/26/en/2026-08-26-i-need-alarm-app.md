## Idea

Shift-Cycle Alarm
For shift patterns such as two days on, two days off, set up one cycle once and have alarms schedule themselves indefinitely.

## Product concept

Starting from a chosen date, users draw a “ring, ring, off, off” cycle and preview the actual alarm dates for the coming weeks. A temporary shift change affects only that cycle, leaving the schedule that follows intact.
