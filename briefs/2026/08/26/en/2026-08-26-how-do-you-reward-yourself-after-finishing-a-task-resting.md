## Idea

One Chapter per Task
Completing a linked task unlocks just one chapter; at the chapter’s end, reading locks again to keep a reward from becoming a binge.

## Product concept

After you complete a Todoist task, the extension unlocks only the next chapter of the linked work. Once you reach the end of that chapter, it locks the table of contents and autoplay again, so a reward does not turn into an entire night of binge-reading.
