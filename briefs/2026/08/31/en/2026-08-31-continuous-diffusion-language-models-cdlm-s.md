## Idea

Self-Correcting Livestream Captions
For technical livestreams, captions revise entire sentences as later context arrives and flag unstable words, preventing an initially misheard term from being carried forward.

## Product concept

Technical livestream captions render uncertain words in subtly animated text, then revise the full sentence as later context arrives. Terms are locked into the final captions only once they are stable, so viewers can see which content is still being recognized.
