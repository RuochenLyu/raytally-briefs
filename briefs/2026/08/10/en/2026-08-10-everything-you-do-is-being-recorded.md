## Idea

Unrecordable Workspace
Before a demo or meeting screen share, turn sensitive windows into a workspace you can see but recordings cannot.

## Product concept

Before sharing their screen, users designate chats, password managers, and private windows as visible only to them. Recording software captures a fixed mask instead, and the isolation ends automatically when the meeting does.
