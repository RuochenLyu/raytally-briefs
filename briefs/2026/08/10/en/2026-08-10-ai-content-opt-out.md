## Idea

Web-Wide AI Opt-Out Layer
While you browse social and content platforms, it applies each site’s available opt-out settings and hides clearly labeled AI ads, replies, and summaries.

## Product concept

A browser extension that translates “I don’t want to see AI content” into each platform’s existing opt-out settings and hiding rules. When generative summaries, ads, or bot replies cannot be disabled, it leaves a placeholder explaining what was hidden.
