## Idea

Trail Race Audio Overlay Alerts
An ultramarathon companion that layers distance- or time-based voice cues over a race, ducking music and podcasts instead of interrupting them.

## Product concept

Before the race, runners tie aid-station stops, medication, and cutoff times to specific distances or elapsed times. When a cue is due, their headphones briefly duck the music for a spoken reminder, then immediately restore playback; offline, the watch switches to vibration alerts.
