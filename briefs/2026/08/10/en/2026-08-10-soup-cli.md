## Idea

Overnight Laptop Fine-Tuning Queue
Developers with only a standard laptop can queue fine-tuning jobs before bed, let them run safely during idle hours, and test the resulting model the next day.

## Product concept

Before bed, developers add private data and a model to a local training queue. The laptop fine-tunes automatically while plugged in and idle; if it heats up, sleeps, or is needed again, training pauses and resumes from a checkpoint.
