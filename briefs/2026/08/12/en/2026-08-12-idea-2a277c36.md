## Idea

Post-Show Restaurant Traffic Gate
A traffic-control tool for small restaurants after a TV appearance, throttling calls, direct messages, and reservations so a sudden wave of attention becomes manageable demand.

## Product concept

When a small restaurant appears on a TV show, it switches its phone, direct messages, reservations, and delivery channels into throttled mode. Customers are routed to available dates, a waitlist, or a sold-out response, so staff only handle orders the restaurant can actually fulfill.
