## Idea

Honest Sleep Streaks
When an automatic sleep log is missing and a streak is about to break, the app verifies device signals, separates missed recordings from false backfills, and offers an explainable path to recovery.

## Product concept

When a night is missing from the sleep log, the app uses phone inactivity, charging, and wearable data to distinguish a device recording failure from a suspicious backfill. Missed recordings can restore the streak, while suspicious entries become recovery days that do not fabricate sleep.
