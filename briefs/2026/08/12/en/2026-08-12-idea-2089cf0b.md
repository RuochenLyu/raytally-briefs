## Idea

Job Application Reply Agent
After an application is submitted, an agent captures recruiter replies, sends one follow-up when needed, and surfaces only the items that require the job seeker’s attention.

## Product concept

When a job seeker submits an application, they automatically receive a role-specific email alias, so confirmation emails, assessments, and interview invitations all land in one place. If there is still no response after an agreed number of days, the product can send one follow-up in a pre-authorized tone.
