## Idea

Squeeze-Deep Sauce Cup
Stacks as a shallow cup for takeout, then squeezes into a deeper sauce well before eating so food gets fully coated in one dip without drips on the table.

## Product concept

The sauce cup stays shallow and stackable for packing. Before eating, squeeze the sides to collapse the walls into a narrow, deeper sauce well. It works with existing lids and packing racks, giving fries or chicken nuggets enough sauce in a single dip.
