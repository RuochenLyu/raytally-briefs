## Idea

Reversible-Grip Headband
After washing your hair, flip the headband to its high-grip lining to hold smooth hair in place for skincare or exercise without tightening it further.

## Product concept

The headband has a reversible lining: on wash days, turn to the silicone microdot side to grip freshly washed, slippery hair; the next day, flip to the lower-pull side. The lining is removable for washing and replacement, and the package includes a simple slip test.
