## Idea

Spoiler-Free Asynchronous Watch Room
When friends and family cannot watch a match at the same time, their reactions appear alongside the replay moment by moment, preserving the shared excitement without spoilers.

## Product concept

Friends and family who watch first attach cheers and commentary to specific moments in the match. Those who open a licensed replay later hear each reaction only when the footage reaches that moment, so the score is never spoiled.
