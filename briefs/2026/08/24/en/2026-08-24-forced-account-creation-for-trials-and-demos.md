## Idea

Disposable Trial Identity
Enter mandatory free trials or demos with a revocable temporary identity, then reclaim your data and cut off marketing access when the session ends.

## Product concept

When a trial page requires registration, the extension generates an email alias and temporary credentials for that session. Once the trial ends, the user can revoke the alias and send unsubscribe and account-deletion requests with one click.
