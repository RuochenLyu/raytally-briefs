## Idea

Adaptive Goal Calendar
Turn long-term goals into scheduled calendar work blocks, then automatically replan what remains when you fall behind.

## Product concept

After users enter a goal, deadline, and milestones, the product schedules work blocks in their existing calendar. If a task is missed, it reschedules the remaining plan and identifies the milestone most likely to slip first.
