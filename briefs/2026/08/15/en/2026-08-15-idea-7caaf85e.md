## Idea

Bring-Your-Own-Stream Watch Party
A remote watch room where friends play their own authorized streams while the app synchronizes playback across platforms and supports live chat.

## Product concept

Friends each play a movie through a platform they are authorized to use, while the room syncs only playback controls, chat, and scene position. When ads, opening credits, or different cuts cause viewers to drift apart, scene anchors bring everyone back into sync.
