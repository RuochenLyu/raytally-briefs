## Idea

One Bird at a Time
At the park, beginners take on one local bird and use audio clues to make their first independent sighting.

## Product concept

When birdwatching beginners arrive at a park, they first choose one common local bird to look for. Through their headphones, they receive only calls and visual clues; when they hear the target bird, the app tells them whether to look in the canopy, on the water, or in the undergrowth—so their first sighting is one they make themselves.
