## Idea

Shared Ticket Queue Handoff
Coordinates multiple people queueing for scarce event tickets in real time, assigning sessions and buyers so the group avoids duplicate purchases or missing out altogether.

## Product concept

When several people are queueing for tickets to a scarce event, the extension reads each person’s queue progress and available sessions. Once someone reaches the purchase page, everyone else immediately knows whether to keep waiting, switch dates, or leave the queue.
