## Idea

Collaborative Caption Review
When automated captions struggle with names and industry terms, short clips are routed to knowledgeable colleagues for confirmation and compiled into a publish-ready version.

## Product concept

Once automated captions are generated, the product isolates only low-confidence names and industry terms into clips a few seconds long. A colleague familiar with the material confirms them once, and each correction is applied across the full video.
