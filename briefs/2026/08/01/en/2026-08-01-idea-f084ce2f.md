## Idea

Dose-Change Refill Handoff
When a dose change leaves a medication supply running low, submit the original prescription and new order to see the run-out date and generate handoff materials for the prescriber and pharmacy.

## Product concept

Users upload their original prescription, the dose-change message, and their remaining supply. The product calculates when they will run out at the new dose, then creates separate handoff notes for the prescriber and pharmacy. Replies from both sides flow into one page, clarifying who needs to update the prescription and who needs to confirm the dispensing date.
