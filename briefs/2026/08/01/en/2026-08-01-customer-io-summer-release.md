## Idea

Automatic Rescheduling for Late Service Visits
When a service appointment is likely to run late, customers receive advance options to wait or reschedule, and both calendars update automatically.

## Product concept

After connecting the appointment calendar and employees’ travel schedules, the system forecasts how late a service visit is likely to be. For short delays, it asks the customer to arrive a little later; for longer ones, it offers alternative time slots. Once the customer chooses, both calendars update automatically.
