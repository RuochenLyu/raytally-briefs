## Idea

Wet Court Cleanup Crew
When you arrive to find a waterlogged court, take a photo to see how many people and which tools are needed, then rally nearby players to clear it and start playing.

## Product concept

Anyone who finds a waterlogged court uploads a full-court photo. The product estimates the people and equipment needed to clear it, along with when play can begin. Nearby players enter when they can arrive and whether they can bring a court squeegee. Once the required threshold is met, everyone receives a notification to head over.
