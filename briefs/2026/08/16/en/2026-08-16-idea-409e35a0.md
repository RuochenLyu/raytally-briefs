## Idea

Set Boundaries Before the Challenge
Two creators set private boundaries before filming, then receive progressively bolder challenges and round-by-round clips ready to post.

## Product concept

Before filming, each creator privately sets boundaries that cannot be crossed. The product escalates prompts only within the range both accept, and either person can stop the escalation by holding down the screen.
