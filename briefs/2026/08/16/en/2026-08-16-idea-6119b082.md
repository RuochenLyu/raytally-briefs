## Idea

Keep Posts Public, Pause New Followers
Keeps posts public during unwanted attention or harassment while automatically removing new followers for the duration of a pause.

## Product concept

When a public account suddenly draws unwanted attention, its owner can freeze the existing follower list. Any new followers added during the pause are automatically removed, while the owner can still approve people they know from a pending queue.
