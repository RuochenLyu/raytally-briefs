## Idea

Segmented Dictation for Long-Form Writing
Dictate in segments when you do not want to type continuously; the product fits the fragments into an outline and asks you to resolve only the awkward transitions.

## Product concept

Users dictate an article in segments, and the system places each segment in the appropriate part of an outline, removing only spoken repetitions and obvious transcription errors. When paragraphs do not flow smoothly, it highlights the joins and offers transition-sentence options.
