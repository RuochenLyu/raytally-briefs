## Idea

Short-Video Swipe Bill
During work breaks, it counts each short-video swipe and uses a recall test and a session bill at the limit to help you stop.

## Product concept

Each swipe to the next short video is counted. Once the user-set limit is reached, the product shows thumbnails of recently watched videos and asks the user to pick three they remember. At the end, it compares the number of swipes and actual time spent with the intended break length.
