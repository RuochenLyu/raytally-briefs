## Idea

Use Card Benefits Along the Way
As a credit card benefit is about to reset, it combines nearby eligible perks into a short trip you can complete along the way.

## Product concept

The product identifies dining credits, ride-share allowances, and airport benefits that are about to reset, then arranges them into a short route based on the user’s weekend plans and nearby locations. Benefits that are not worth making a special trip for are tucked away.
