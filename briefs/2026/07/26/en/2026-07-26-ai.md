## Idea

AI Work Trial Ledger
Before renewing or promoting an AI tool, run personal A/B tests on real tasks to see where it genuinely saves time and where it creates more rework.

## Product concept

Teams alternate between AI-assisted and non-AI approaches for similar tasks, recording time spent and rework. After two weeks, they review each task to identify real gains, ineffective uses, and cases where AI produced a net loss.
