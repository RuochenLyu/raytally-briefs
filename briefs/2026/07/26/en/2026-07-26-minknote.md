## Idea

De-identified Copies of Meeting Notes
After documenting a sensitive meeting, generate a local de-identified copy with consistent aliases and intact links for a supervisor or colleague to review.

## Product concept

Practitioners continue writing sensitive meeting notes in local Markdown files, while the app creates a de-identified copy with consistent aliases. Before export, it shows only the differences between the original and the copy, making omissions easier to catch.
