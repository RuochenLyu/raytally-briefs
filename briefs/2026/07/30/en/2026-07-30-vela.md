## Idea

Candidate Interview Itinerary
During a packed interview cycle, candidates can forward invitations from each company to automatically organize their schedule, spot conflicts, and draft rescheduling replies for approval.

## Product concept

After candidates forward interview invitations from multiple companies, the product consolidates time zones, meeting links, and preparation items in one place. It flags conflicts and overly short transitions, then proposes rescheduling combinations with the least impact; replies are sent only after the user confirms.
