## Idea

Acceptance-to-Invoice
After a project is delivered, it finds acceptance evidence in client communications and immediately creates an invoice package that is easier to approve and pay.

## Product concept

A design firm or independent consultant adds delivery links and client communications to a project card. The product identifies acceptance evidence such as “live” or “ready to close,” then checks it against the contract and generates an invoice package with a delivery checklist and payment details.
