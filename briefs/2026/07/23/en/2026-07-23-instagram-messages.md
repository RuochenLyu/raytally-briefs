## Idea

Social Media Outage Backup Inbox
When social media DMs go down, an independent inbox page automatically takes over inquiries, bookings, and customer-service messages for small businesses.

## Product concept

Small businesses that rely on Instagram DMs for orders set up an independent short link in advance. When widespread DM disruptions are detected, the link automatically switches to a page for messages, inquiries, and bookings; once service returns, the owner transfers items from the temporary inbox back to the original channel one by one.
