## Idea

Make Sense of an Option Offer
Paste in an equity offer and the company’s claims to see the cash required, potential returns, and terms worth questioning.

## Product concept

Job seekers paste in an equity offer and the company’s public claims. Using adjustable assumptions, the tool breaks down the strike price, vesting schedule, post-termination exercise window, dilution, and taxes. It shows the worst-case cash outlay, after-tax proceeds at different exit prices, and the questions to raise in the next negotiation.
