## Idea

Make Room Before You Buy
Before placing an order, compare the item with alternatives at home, see its true incremental cost, and revisit your reason for buying after a cooling-off period.

## Product concept

When they want to buy something, users forward the product link and the product first looks for older items at home with similar functions. They must choose one item to discard, resell, or give away before seeing the true incremental cost, expected number of uses, and how their reason for buying changes after a cooling-off period.
