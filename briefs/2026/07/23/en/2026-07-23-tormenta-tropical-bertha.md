Storm-Ready Store Plans
Before a storm, enter store and staffing details to get closure timing, employee contact order, and ready-to-send notifications.

## Product concept

Once a storm warning is issued, owners enter each store’s location, operating hours, employee commuting routes, and refrigeration equipment. The page lays out closure timing, contact order, and ready-to-send notifications; when the official forecast changes, it flags only the schedules and cold-chain decisions that need to be revisited.
