## Idea

Practice Before a Bear Encounter
A few-minute bear-encounter drill before entering bear country reveals mistakes in drawing spray, positioning, and retreating.

## Product concept

Before entering bear country, hikers use their phones to run through short scenarios involving a bend in the trail, cubs, or a campsite. Each round requires a timed choice of action and practice drawing bear spray; afterward, it flags problems with positioning, retreat order, or taking too long to retrieve the spray.
