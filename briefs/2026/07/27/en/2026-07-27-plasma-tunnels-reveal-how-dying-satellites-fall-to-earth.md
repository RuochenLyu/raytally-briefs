## Idea

Satellite Reentry Observation Relay
Coordinates observers along a satellite’s reentry path to turn scattered night-sky videos into an increasingly precise trajectory.

## Product concept

As a satellite nears reentry, observers along its projected path receive local viewing directions, time windows, and filming guidance. After participants upload night-sky footage, the system calibrates the trajectory against stars and narrows the next observation window.
