## Idea

Expiring Memory for AI
Share personal context with multiple AI services under defined purposes and expiration dates, injecting only the memories needed for the task at hand.

## Product concept

Users assign an intended use and expiration date to facts such as their résumé and dietary restrictions. Before each conversation, they preview exactly what will be sent, and expired information is automatically no longer injected. The first version logs each service’s access trail.
