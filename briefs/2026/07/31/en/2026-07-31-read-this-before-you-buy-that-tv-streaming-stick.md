First-Connect TV Streaming Stick Checkup
Before a low-cost TV streaming stick joins your home network, run it through an isolated check to get an immediate safety verdict, router settings, and evidence for a return.

## Product concept

When a low-cost TV streaming stick first connects to the internet, the product places it on a restricted network and tests playback. It checks for unfamiliar network connections, dangerous ports, and fake resolution claims. If it finds a problem, it generates isolation steps and an evidence page for a return.
