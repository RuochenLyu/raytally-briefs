## Idea

Breaktime Craft Relay Cart
During phone-free school breaks, middle and high school students turn physical dials to pick a time-appropriate craft activity they can do alone or pass on to the next student.

## Product concept

During phone-free breaks, schools set out an activity cart where students turn two physical dials—“How many minutes do you have?” and “On your own or with others?”—to receive a short craft prompt. They can hang partially completed projects back in numbered slots for the next student to continue.
