## Idea

Consent-First Reaction Camera
Records unexpected reactions, then seals the footage until the person filmed can approve, trim, or delete it; it can be shared only with both parties’ consent.

## Product concept

After an unexpected reaction is recorded, the video is immediately encrypted and sealed; the person filming cannot export or upload it. The person filmed previews it on their phone and can approve, trim, or delete it. Only the version both parties confirm is unlocked for sharing.
