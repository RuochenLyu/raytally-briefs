## Idea

Open-Source App Sponsorship Wallet
After an app store removes donation links, a monthly wallet automatically allocates a user’s contribution across the open-source apps they continue to use.

## Product concept

Users set a monthly budget, and the sponsorship wallet automatically divides it among the open-source apps they actively use. Projects publish their payment addresses in signed manifests, and users can review where that month’s money will go before payment.
