## Idea

Long Reads for E-Readers
Send an article and a question to a friend’s e-reader, then receive their highlights as a reply after they finish reading.

## Product concept

Package a long-form article, an opening note, and a question as an e-book to send to a friend. When they finish reading, their highlights and brief annotations are collected into a reply letter, without pressuring them to respond right away.
