## Idea

Blind Translation Readings of the Odyssey
When discussing new translations of classics, readers anonymously choose between translated passages before seeing the group’s disagreements and the trade-offs behind each version.

## Product concept

A book club chooses a short passage from old and new translations of the Odyssey, hides the translators’ names, and asks everyone to vote blind and mark the words or lines that move them. Only after the vote are the translators’ backgrounds, the structure of the original text, and the trade-offs behind each translation revealed.
