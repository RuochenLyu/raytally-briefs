Synchronized Rehearsal Lyrics
During band rehearsals, members share lyrics synced to the chosen recording, so a lead singer’s edit keeps everyone aligned.

## Product concept

Once a band selects the recording version for rehearsal, every member opens the same word-by-word lyrics on their phone. When the lead singer corrects a lyric or adjusts an entry cue, the change appears instantly on everyone’s screen.
