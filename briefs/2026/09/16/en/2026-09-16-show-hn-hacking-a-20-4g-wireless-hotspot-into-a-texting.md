## Idea

Low-Cost On-Site Texting Box
When a worksite does not want to issue smartphones, a low-cost 4G box lets the team exchange text messages and check who is online.

## Product concept

For temporary communications at construction sites or events, flash an inexpensive 4G hotspot to turn it into a texting device. Phones and dedicated devices with physical buttons can join the same group, while an administrator can also see which devices are online.
