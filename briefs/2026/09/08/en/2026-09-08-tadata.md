## Idea

Asynchronous Voting Without Groupthink
Before a team discusses an important proposal, gather each member’s true position independently, then reveal the disagreements that actually exist.

## Product concept

For important proposals in Slack, collect each person’s support, concerns, and conditions privately first. Reveal the disagreements only after the deadline, so the first speaker cannot set the tone.
