## Idea

Smart TV Privacy Firewall
When a smart TV joins the home network, it automatically reduces unnecessary tracking while confirming that streaming and casting still work.

## Product concept

Once privacy rules are enabled for a smart TV at the router, it first blocks advertising identifiers and viewing-data reporting. If playback or casting stops working, it rolls back only the rule causing the problem.
