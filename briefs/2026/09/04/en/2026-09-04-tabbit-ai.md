## Idea

Lend an Agent a Browser Tab
When an agent needs to complete a task on a signed-in website, lend it one browser tab temporarily and have access automatically revoked when the task is done.

## Product concept

Users temporarily lend an agent a single tab where they are already signed in. The user still confirms payments, publishing, and deletions themselves, and access expires automatically when the task ends or times out.
