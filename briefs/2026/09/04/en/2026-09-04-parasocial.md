## Idea

Podcast Listening Relay
When friends hear a podcast moment worth discussing, they can start a relay with a voice note explaining why, ending in a shared listening conversation among multiple people.

## Product concept

A friend clips a podcast segment and records a 10-second note on why it is worth sharing. The next person must respond before choosing another segment to pass along, gradually building a shared listening conversation among multiple people.
